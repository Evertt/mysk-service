## 2. Numbered inconsistencies

1. **Page titles: five treatments.** `PageHeader` (h1 text-2xl, page-header.svelte:76), hand-rolled h1 (dashboard, portal pages), `ToolbarBand` h1 text-xl (equipment, schedule), `PageBanner` (docks, condition, checklists), energy `HeroBanner`. Majority/best: PageHeader. Severity: high (already item 1 in code-system.md; confirmed visually: equipment and docks look like different apps).
2. **Teal means everything.** Solid teal fills for: primary buttons, ToolbarBand, PageBanner gradient, portal progress bar and "Spend parts credit", logo tile, active nav; teal tint for: nav active, default badge, "Enabled since" is info-blue, filter chip "All 514", focus ring; teal 2px bar before EVERY section-card title (lagoon.ts:122-131), also on nested tiles and login. Result: nothing stands out as the call to action; the equipment page has the largest teal area (a 150px-high band) next to an unthemed grey table. Severity: high. See recommendation 1.
3. **Card-title size flips with card structure.** Header+content cards: text-sm (14px) with a teal bar; header-only cards: text-base (16px) (lagoon.ts:122-126; html shows both utility classes). Login "Sign in", dashboard widgets, docks "Notices" are 14px; energy site card title "Riedel Ede - Productielocatie" is 14px bold and looks smaller than the page's own table text on some pages. Severity: med.
4. **Primary button contrast.** `--primary` oklch(.6 .118 184.7) ~ teal-600 (#009689) with white text = ~~3.7:1, below WCAG AA 4.5:1 for 14px semibold text (login Sign in, New order, New unit). Hover darkens 14%. Severity: med (accessibility). Use teal-700 (L~~.51) for fills or dark text.
5. **Three corner radii in one screen.** `--radius .625rem`; cards `rounded-md` (8px), badges 4px (`rounded-[0.25rem]`), buttons/inputs lg (10px), dialogs rounded-lg, banners rounded-lg, pills (chips) rounded-full. On equipment: pill chips + 4px badges + 8px table + 10px buttons. Severity: low-med. Pick: 8px containers, 6px controls, 4px badges, pills only for counts/avatars.
6. **Sidebar ordering/naming is not a model.** "Records" contains modules (Work orders, Components, Docks, Maintenance plans, Parts shop) and "Modules" contains Invoicing, Energy, Service contracts, Partner programme. "Work orders" (checklists module) next to "Orders" (core) is confusing. Severity: med.
7. **Top bar clutter / dev tools in prod chrome.** Bug icon, language, theme picker, colour mode: 4 controls + bell, none labelled; theme picker is shown to all users (login too). Severity: med. Move language/theme/colour into the user menu, keep bell + feedback.
8. **Error pages.** 403 and 404 share one template but: 404 has no shell; titles "Something went wrong" for 403 and "Not found" twice for 404; "Reload" offered on both; icon colours neutral. Severity: med (rare pages, but they undermine trust; easy to fix).
9. **Dashboard tile row.** 9 tiles / 7 columns leaves 2 orphans; "View" button on 4 of 9 tiles; icon colours arbitrary; tiles are `stacked` StatTile (code-system 10). On mobile 9 full-width tiles. Severity: med-high (the first screen of the app).
10. **Dashboard widget cards use 4+ internal layouts and masonry columns of unequal height**, blank chart cards leave gaps, header actions differ in wording ("All orders", "View all", "All activity", "View"). Severity: med.
11. **Settings page**: nested bordered rows inside a section card, "Enabled since" info badge on all rows, "Set up" button missing on 3 of 11 rows, chips that look interactive but are read-only, subtitle is just the org name. Severity: med. Settings sub-pages exist in 8 different module-specific layouts (max-w-2xl... 5xl, see code-system 12).
12. **Notifications popover**: all-bold sentences, no severity/unread/grouping, 17 identical items. Severity: low-med.
13. **Sidebar details**: order count is bare text (not a badge) and is the only live count; no counts for alerts/overdue; mobile drawer footer overlaps list. Severity: low.
14. **Type scale drift between office and portals**: office tables 14px/row 53px, portal list rows 16px bold titles; section headings in portal ("Open orders · 26", "Recently completed") are small grey text, not card/section titles; the same concept looks different. Severity: med.
15. **Muted subtitle is 16px** under a 24px h1 (page-header.svelte:77): large and wide (energy: full-width 120-character line). Compare card descriptions at 12px. Severity: low. Suggest 14px, max-w-prose.
16. **Dark navy card** (portal wallet) and the dark "PLATINUM" chip are the only dark surfaces in a light theme; a third visual idiom. Severity: low-med.

## 3. Worth keeping

- The login card: restrained, section-card header with teal bar, muted input fills, one solid primary. Use it as the template for all auth.
- The lagoon base: cool slate page, white cards with hairline borders and no shadows, muted-filled inputs that turn white on focus, header-band section cards, uppercase 11px sidebar group labels, pale-teal active nav item. Calm and professional; reads well in tables and forms.
- Tinted status badges with a hairline in their hue (small, square-ish): the best status idiom in the app; dense and scannable. Keep, but ensure 12px minimum text.
- Collapsible icon-rail sidebar and the org switcher header.
- The 403 within the shell (sidebar kept) and the empty-state style (icon square, title, muted line, actions): good pattern, fix copy only.
- Dashboard "Receivables" card (big figures with a red overdue number) and "Overdue invoices" table: the most informative widgets.
- Docks' status KPI row (label above big coloured number, status colour only on the number) is clearer than the dashboard tiles.

## 4. Recommendations

1. **Lagoon token direction.** Teal = brand + the ONE primary action per page + active nav + focus ring + links. Do NOT use it for: large surfaces (bands, banners, hero gradients), decoration (the title bar on every card, tile icon colours), or status (statuses use success/warning/info/destructive/neutral only). Darken the fill token (primary ~ oklch(.52 .105 186), teal-700) so white text passes 4.5:1; keep the current .6 teal as `--brand`/sidebar-primary for logo and bars. Reserve the card-title bar for page-level section cards or drop it; use the 14px bold title with a divider instead.
2. **Retire `ToolbarBand`, `PageBanner` and the hand-rolled h1s in favour of `PageHeader`** (plus optional eyebrow/icon slot); a band is the single biggest teal surface and breaks the scale. Dashboard, portals and module pages should call PageHeader.
3. **Fix the type scale in tokens**: h1 24/600, card title 14/600 everywhere (set the header-only card to the same size), subtitle/description 14 muted with max-w-prose, body 14, meta 12, badge/label minimum 12px (no 11px/10px). Portal rows should use the same 14px row style as office tables. Radii: card 8, control 6-8, badge 4, pill only for chips/avatars.
4. **Tidy the shell**: group the sidebar by user task (Work: Orders, Schedule, Work orders, Activity; Customers & equipment; Stock & shop; Finance), put language/theme/colour mode in the user menu, turn the order count into a pill, and make 403/404 proper: lock/search icon, distinct titles ("You don't have access", "Page not found"), one primary button "Back to dashboard", no Reload, 404 inside the shell when signed in.
5. **Dashboard**: 4-5 KPI tiles (orders, awaiting, urgent, revenue, needs attention), same tile for all, whole tile clickable (drop the "View" buttons), 2-up on mobile; widgets in a 2-column grid with equal-height rows and one header-action pattern ("View all"); settings: modules as a plain list/table with a toggle and "Configure", no nested boxes.
