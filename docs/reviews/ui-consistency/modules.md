# Review: module dashboards and settings pages (reviewer `modules`)

Looked at desktop screenshots of all pages in scope; the mobile shots of schedule and energy-site are 12,000 px tall single columns (unreadable at review size), so mobile notes come from source/structure only.

## 1. Per-page notes

**schedule** (modules/schedule/routes/(app)/+page.svelte:32, ToolbarBand). Teal band with icon, "69 orders" count pill, description, prev/next + Today + Month/Week/Day segmented control inside the band. The band is the page's real toolbar (date navigation, view switch), so here it has a genuine job; the board under it is a white card grid with a right-hand backlog column. Problems: backlog column is a 41-item stack that makes the page 4,600 px tall; day columns are ~90 px wide, so order cards truncate customer names ("Van den Bos...", "Bouwgroep ..."). The orange/teal/red left stripe colours on cards come from type-colors.ts (27 raw palette classes). On mobile the board becomes a day-by-day list (very long).

**docks** (PageBanner with eyebrow "SALVO SYSTEM", badges "SIMULATION" and customer, `Salvo feed` secondary button). Page then stacks: a bordered "Salvo simulation" bar, 5 stat tiles (4/4, 4, 0, 4, 01:26), a 2-col grid of dock cards each holding a truck SVG illustration, three bordered mini-boxes, a safety sub-box, a key/value list, a hint box and "Recent sessions", then "Docks without Salvo" chips, Notices card, two explainer cards ("How Salvo secures the dock", "Signals by door-open time") and a "components at the dock" card. It is a product demo page more than an app page: ~2,900 px, 6 different box styles (Card, bordered mini-box, dashed hint, chip, tile, Empty). Card titles have the lagoon teal bar; the dock cards have a title in uppercase "DOCK 05" plus a badge "Loading".

**docks-settings** and **energy-settings**: ModuleSettingsPage-style header (breadcrumb, h1, description) then two Cards at ~900 px wide ("Integration tokens", "Docks"/"Sites and codes"). Identical to each other, good. But the "Only owners and admins make and revoke tokens." note sits in the card body as body text, while on the other settings pages it is a card header description (see 2.6).

**energy** (index): PageHeader, then one site card (a single 370 px card in an otherwise empty 1440 px page; card has only 0 kW / 0 kWh although the site page shows 507 kW: the index shows "Actual power 0 kW" for a site with 24 open alerts, i.e. the index reads different data or no live reading). The grid of cards has no table alternative.

**energy-site** (HeroBanner at modules/energy/overview/hero-banner.svelte). Hero with breadcrumbs inside the banner, white text, chips "ENERGY MODULE", "SIMULATION 30x", site pill, title, description and three header buttons (Alerts / Thresholds / Energy report). Under it: SimulationBar, a row of FilterChips (whole building, Begane grond, 1e verdieping, Kelder) with a SegmentedNav ("Today vs yesterday | Month vs last month") pushed right, six KpiTiles, a dark slate "Building overview" diagram (slate-800 header, teal-400 text), a door-watch row of four cards with door SVGs, a full open-alerts list (24 rows, 1,400 px of identical text), hourly consumption chart + detail panel, a zone comparison table, and a Signals / Savings advice pair. It is 6,500 px (12,000 px on mobile). The building overview and door cards are the best "visual" content in the app, but the alerts list in the middle pushes the charts three screens down.

**energy-site-alerts**: PageHeader + FilterChips (All alerts / Open / Order made / Resolved) + card list. Same alert rows as the site page, so it is an exact duplicate of the "Open alerts" block, which has "All alerts" link. Status tokens "Active"/"Near" are red/amber badges (OK, EnumBadge). Chips here are a filter on a list, which is a legit use but is the 4th list-filter style (chips, not Filter menu).

**energy-zone**: breadcrumb (3 levels), h1 "Z-LAB · Laboratorium", status badge pushed to the far right of the subtitle row (floating, no relation to the title), 4 tiles with inconsistent heights (two have a divider and a footer, two have only a value: a Card.Header/Content tiles using lagoon's section-card look for a KPI), bar chart in raw orange/blue (not theme teal), consumers table. Chart colours (orange `#f59e0b`-ish and sky blue) are used on every energy chart and match no token; the rest of the app uses teal.

**energy-site-thresholds**: PageHeader, max-w-4xl, then 8 cards each with form fields shown as read-only DetailItems for a planner (so a planner sees a 900 px-wide stack of 8 near-identical cards with nothing to do). Note "Only owners and admins change the thresholds." is plain text between description and cards: third placement of the same notice.

**energy-site-report**: no PageHeader. Starts with a bare "Energy / Riedel Ede" slash breadcrumb (plain text, different from the real Breadcrumb component used one page above), a date-range form, a teal "Export PDF" button (the only solid-teal button in module pages), then a document-style heading (small uppercase eyebrow, h1, meta). `max-w-5xl` (1024 px) so it left-aligns with a 135 px gap on the right at 1440. Section h2s are ~text-xl, bigger than card titles. Tables are bare, unboxed; the one chart is in a Card. This is a print document inside the app shell, which is defensible, but its header/breadcrumb is not the standard one.

**energy-site-setup**: 403 for the planner; not reviewable.

**condition-settings, maintenance-settings, invoices-settings**: ModuleSettingsPage: breadcrumb, h1, description, one `max-w-2xl` (672 px) card with a note line "Only owners and admins can change these settings." as the first row, then a 3-up DetailItem grid. Consistent with each other. Odd: they show a full-width empty page (card uses 47 % of 1440) and the note is addressed to a user who is looking at a read-only view; the view gives no hint of who can edit or any edit button (a manager sees the form).

**parts-shop-settings** ("Shop categories"): breadcrumb parent is "Shop products", a plain `Card.Root class="max-w-2xl"` with rows (name + count) and the notice as a footer line. Does not use ModuleSettingsPage. Title/h1 "Shop categories" but nav item is "Parts shop" and the settings URL is `/parts-shop/settings`; unclear it is the shop's settings.

**partner-programme** (list): PageHeader + ListLayout table; has a Level column using the metal badges (PLATINUM dark solid, BRONZE salmon, "No level" outline) and a progress bar column. Progress bar column is a good idea (revenue "€204,263 of €250,000" 81 %) and should become a generic table cell. Level badge is uppercase 11 px while every other badge in the app is title case (see 2.9). Only 3 of 12 rows have a level, so the column reads mostly "No level".

**partner-programme-settings** ("Programme years"): breadcrumb, `max-w-2xl` card containing a bare table; table header band sits inside card padding (inset gray header with a card border around it: card-in-card look). **partner-programme-year**: `max-w-4xl`-ish (900 px) two cards with tables; "Close year" outline button sits top-right at the page edge (x = 1316-1416), far from the 900 px content; the actions slot is at the shell edge while content is narrow, so the button floats unattached. Level badges: bronze/silver/gold/gold+/platinum/platinum+/elite/elite+ in 4 unrelated colour families (salmon, grey, yellow, black, blue) while "No level" is white/outline; they look like the only hard-coded palette in the table UI, though meaningful. **-year-close**: not viewed in detail.

**checklists-settings** (Checklist templates): breadcrumb, h1, a FULL-WIDTH card (1136 px) with a table (name, norm, valid for, category chips, points); no actions (a "New template" button is absent for this role, or missing). Category chips are outlined square pills, not Badge variants used elsewhere (secondary = blue tint). Doesn't use ModuleSettingsPage, and is a list, so it has no search/Filter/sort, unlike every other list.

## 2. Numbered inconsistencies

1. **Module landing pages use four different headers** (high). PageHeader (energy index, partner-programme, maintenance, parts-shop, invoices, contracts, materials), ToolbarBand (schedule), PageBanner (docks, condition, checklists work-orders overview), HeroBanner (energy-site), and on energy-report a hand-rolled one. Which header a module gets is decided by whoever copied the mockup, not by page type. Best: PageHeader (about 50 pages). Files: toolbar-band.svelte, page-banner.svelte, energy/overview/hero-banner.svelte:69.

2. **Settings pages share a layout only in 5 of 11 cases** (high). `ModuleSettingsPage` (lib/modules/settings/module-settings-page.svelte; breadcrumb, PageHeader, max-w-2xl card with DetailItem grid or a form) serves invoices, maintenance, condition, energy, docks. NOT using it: parts-shop settings (own max-w-2xl Card, parts-shop/routes/(app)/settings/+page.svelte:37), partner-programme settings (max-w-2xl Card, :38), partner-programme year (900 px), checklists settings (full-width card), energy thresholds (max-w-4xl, :25), energy setup (max-w-5xl, :56). Widths on the same "settings" family: 672 px, 900 px, 1024 px, 1136 px.

3. **Where the "Only owners and admins ..." notice lives** (med). As the first row of the card (invoices/maintenance/condition), as Card.Description under a title (partner year "Levels", docks/energy token card), as body text in the card (docks/energy tokens: "Only owners and admins make and revoke tokens."), as a footer line (parts-shop categories), as a loose paragraph between description and cards (thresholds). 5 placements, 5 wordings; a planner (non-manager) sees these pages in the screenshots, yet the notice reads as if they were editable. Better: one `ManagersNotice`/`description` slot in the settings layout, shown only when the viewer cannot edit.

4. **Page action placement vs content width** (med). On settings pages the content is 672-900 px but actions (Close year, Energy settings, Programme years) sit at the far right of a 1440 px shell, so they are disconnected from the content (partner-programme-year: button at x=1316, card ends at 1176). Either cap the whole page (PageHeader included) with a max-width or let the content use the full width.

5. **Heading h1 sizes/weights across module pages**: PageHeader text-2xl; ToolbarBand text-xl; hero/banner text-2xl; report h1 text-2xl without tracking-tight and h2 ~text-xl in the same page that has `text-sm` card titles (energy-site-report). Low-med.

6. **Breadcrumbs** (med): module settings/sub pages have a proper breadcrumb above the h1 (condition, maintenance, invoices, docks, energy-zone, thresholds); energy-site has breadcrumbs INSIDE the hero in white; energy-report has a plain slash-separated text ("Energy / Riedel Ede") that is not a link nav; module top pages and banner pages (docks, condition) have none. Back-to-parent on parts-shop settings goes to "Shop products", on checklists to "Work orders", which are not the module's index (energy back goes to the module index) so "Settings" lives at different depths.

7. **Stat tiles: three look-alikes on module pages** (med): StatTile (docks, condition), `KpiTile` (energy-site, hand-rolled, tone via raw class string), and lagoon section-card as tile (energy-zone: Card.Header + Card.Content, two with footers and two without, so tiles in one row have a different internal structure but the same height). Docks tiles: white card, icon + label + coloured big number (4/4 green, 0 red, 4 blue, 01:26 orange): the coloured numbers are decorative (blue "Doors open = 4", green "Locked = 4") so colour does not carry meaning that matches the semantic tokens (a count of open doors is blue/info, a "Not locked" 0 is red even though 0 is good).

8. **Chart colours** (med): energy and docks charts use orange + sky blue (energy-zone, report, site hourly chart) and slate/teal in the building overview, while the app's brand is teal. Orange/blue are not tokens (state-colours spec allows file exceptions). Charts in the dashboard (not my scope) should be checked for the same.

9. **Badge case and style** (med): partner levels are UPPERCASE solid blocks in 4 colour families (partner-programme list, year page) and docks "SALVO", "SIMULATION" labels are uppercase on the banner, while all EnumBadges (Nominal, Exceeded, Nearly reached, Loading) are title case with a tint. Within energy-zone one row mixes "Nominal" (green tint) and "Normal" (green tint with a different word) for the same state on one page (header badge "Nominal", tile badge "Normal").

10. **Page length / density on dashboards** (med): docks (2,900 px; four identical dock cards each with 6 sections + 3 explainer cards that never change), energy-site (6,500 px; alerts list in the middle), schedule (4,600 px: 8 engineers x 7 days + a 41-card backlog). The mockups were demos with explanatory content ("How Salvo secures the dock", "Signals by door-open time", "Savings advice") that does not belong on a daily operations page (empty-Notices card also takes space).

11. **Filter UI on module pages is chips or segmented, never the table Filter menu** (med): energy-site (floor chips + period segmented on one row), alerts (status chips), docks (none), checklists settings table (none), partner-programme (standard Filter). The alerts list is a list without search, sort or pagination (all 37 on one page).

12. **Duplicate content**: energy-site "Open alerts" block is the whole of /energy/[site]/alerts, and the site page header also has an "Alerts" button; zone comparison table on the site page and report page; "Signals" and "Savings advice" cards on the site page and in the report (low-med).

13. **Card styles on the same pages** (med): lagoon section cards (teal tick + title) for most module cards but the docks "Docks without Salvo" chips, door cards, dock mini-boxes and building overview use ad-hoc bordered boxes (`rounded-lg border border-slate-300`, dashed hint boxes), see code-system.md #7, #10, #11. Visible: docks dock-card inner boxes and the energy building overview have different border radius/colour than the outer Card.

14. **Empty / reduced-permission states** (low): energy index shows "0 kW / 0 kWh" for a site that has live data on its own page; docks "Notices: No notices in the last day." takes a full card; partner-programme "No level" pill repeated nine times.

15. **Primary action look** (low-med): report "Export PDF" is solid teal (the only one in this scope), banner pages use `secondary` buttons with a white fill, settings "Close year" is outline, band uses ghost; there are no primary actions on settings pages at all in read-only view.

16. **Mobile** (med, from structure): energy-site has 12,000 px on 390 px (all 6 KPI tiles, door cards, alerts, overview stacked), the building overview diagram is a fixed layout drawing; schedule becomes a vertical list of ~60 cards; chips + segmented control on one row (`ml-auto`) wrap. Docks cards fine (single column).

## 3. Worth keeping

- **Schedule's ToolbarBand** only where the band carries the page's controls (period navigation, view switch, count). It is a real "calendar toolbar"; if generalised, call it what it is (a toolbar for time-based pages, e.g. schedule and dashboard) and do not use it for lists (equipment).
- **PageBanner / HeroBanner as ONE optional "overview hero"** for module landing pages that are dashboards (docks, energy-site, condition, partner portal): eyebrow + badges (live, simulation) + title + intro + actions. One component, `text-primary-foreground`, and used only for dashboards, never for lists or settings.
- **Progress-bar cell** in the partner list (value, "of target", percent): make a table cell `column.progress()`.
- **KPI tile row with semantic tone** (docks, energy, condition): unify to one StatTile layout with a tone prop; use it for energy zone too.
- **Energy building overview + door cards + dock-drawing illustrations**: these are the only places with real personality. They justify one-off, but should be on their own pages/tabs of the module, not stacked with lists, and use tokens.
- **ModuleSettingsPage** (breadcrumb + PageHeader + max-w card + DetailItem/read-only fallback): it is the right settings pattern; extend it to the rest.
- Zone/threshold status badges with the 4-step status (Normal / Nearly reached / Exceeded / Switched off) read very clearly.
- Report as a printable document: good concept, keep (but standard header).

## 4. Recommendations

1. Collapse module headers to two: `PageHeader` everywhere (including equipment, energy report, schedule's title) and one `OverviewHero` (merge PageBanner + HeroBanner, theme tokens, breadcrumbs outside) reserved for dashboard landings (docks, energy site, condition); drop ToolbarBand from lists and keep, if at all, a small calendar-toolbar for schedule.
2. Route every module settings page through ModuleSettingsPage (add slots for "table" / "sections" content), single max-width (e.g. max-w-4xl for the page including the header), one "managers only" notice via the layout; move partner programme years, parts-shop categories, checklist templates, energy thresholds into it.
3. Trim the module dashboards: move explainer cards (docks), alert list (energy-site, link to /alerts) and zone tables to their own pages/tabs; cap the schedule backlog; reduce each dashboard to hero, one tile row, and the main visual.
4. Replace hard-coded palettes in chart/visual files with chart tokens (`--chart-1..n` plus the semantic tones) and one KpiTile = StatTile; add a progress cell and a status-dot component for door/dock states.
5. Settle badge rules: sentence case everywhere (levels as tone badges or a `level` variant), one word per status (Nominal vs Normal), and normalise filters on module lists to the standard Filter menu (alerts, checklist templates).
