# details-forms: detail pages and new/edit forms (office app, lagoon, shltr planner)

Looked at: orders, customers, engineers, equipment (+location), materials, contracts, invoices (detail/new/credit), credit notes (new), parts-shop order, checklists work-order and template detail, and the new forms of orders, customers, equipment, contracts, materials, invoices, credit note; orders-detail and orders-new on mobile.
Overall: this is the most consistent part of the app. Every detail page is `RecordPage` (record-page.svelte) and every create/edit form is `PageHeader` + `RecordForm` section cards. The inconsistencies are in the details below, not in the system.

## 1. Per-page notes

- **orders-detail**: header = number, status badge, priority badge, type (plain text with icon, not a badge), description as subtitle. Main column: Materials and labour, Equipment, Service history. Side column: Schedule, Customer, Site, Engineer, Invoice, Activity (6 cards). Email addresses wrap mid-word ("technischedienst@oost / erhuisvastgoed.exampl / e") in the 2-column key/value grid of Customer and Engineer. The side column is about 1.6x as tall as the main column; the page ends with a large empty area under the main column. Empty "Materials" shows a table with a "No materials on this order." row in the middle of the table; the Invoice card is a card for one sentence.
- **orders-detail mobile**: single column, main first. Schedule (date, the most useful facts) lands below three tables. "Service history" table is clipped at the right edge (status "Comple|", no scroll hint). Edit/Delete drop under the title as two full-height buttons.
- **customers-detail**: only detail page with a 4-tile StatTile row (layout "card"), a 3-line tile ("From 35 completed or invoiced orders") that breaks the row's alignment, plus accent figure colours (teal, amber) that differ per tile. Main: Sites (a card with nested bordered location list), Equipment table, Recent activity, Activity log (second feed, wide, empty). Side: Contact, Portal access, Activity (empty). So there are two "Activity" cards (side "Activity" = this customer, bottom "Activity log" = its units) with the same style and nothing to tell them apart but a subtitle. The equipment table's Status column is clipped by the card edge ("Needs service" touches the border).
- **engineers-detail**: summary row of 3 tiles (the others have 4 or none), Open orders table with 32 rows (no limit, 1,100 px tall), Recent work (10 rows, limited). Profile card repeats Specialty and Status that are already badges in the header. Activity "No activity" empty state takes a full card.
- **equipment-detail**: the only `layout="masonry"` page. 4 big-number tiles (text-3xl, noticeably larger numerals than customers' tiles), then a timeline "Service history" in a wide column and 9 cards flowing in 3 columns (Specifications, Owner, Site, Inspections, Checklist, Condition, Components, Maintenance, Parts used, QR code, Service contracts, Activity). Card order reads left-to-right/top-to-bottom, so related cards (Condition/Components/Maintenance) are interleaved with unrelated ones. Cards in a row end at different heights (ragged bottom edge, by design of masonry). Header badges: status + warranty ("Covered") are both coloured badges.
- **equipment-location**: header has THREE buttons, `Print labels` (outline), `Edit` (outline), `Delete` (outline, neutral). Every other record's Delete is a red-tinted destructive button. QR card repeats "Print label" from the header. The subtitle is "Koelhuis Nijkerk · Productielocatie Deventer" (plain text), whereas the unit page uses links in cards for the same facts. Orders list here is a custom list (no table, no status text size parity), vs the table on order/contract pages.
- **materials-detail**: 9,649 px tall at 1440. "Usage" lists every order that used the material (about 140 rows, no limit or "view all") and "Stock history" lists every movement. Tiles (3), buttons in the header: Receive stock, Count stock, Edit, Delete (four, two of them are outline-with-icon, so the header is busy). Worst page of the scope: the key facts and the side cards (Material, Activity) are scrolled out of view.
- **contracts-detail**: summary replaced by cards: main = Covered equipment (19 rows, unbounded), Orders under this contract (empty state), side = Terms, Customer, Maintenance plans (8 rows), Activity. "Add a plan to each covered unit without one" button overflows its card's right padding (touches border at x=1402 vs. card inner edge at 1396). Header has status and level badges.
- **invoices-detail**: the one "document" detail page: main card is the invoice sheet (company, bill-to, lines, totals) and side cards are forms (Add to the invoice, Add a line, Terms), i.e. inline mini-forms with own "Add line"/"Save terms"/"Add to invoice" buttons (outline, right-aligned). Header has 4 actions: Print, Download PDF (outline), Void (red tinted), Issue (primary teal). Note Issue is the only teal primary on any detail page besides "Ship" on the shop order. Draft badge is grey/neutral outline; lagoon "secondary" looks like a different grey than other statuses. The invoice sheet has no max-width or paper feel (it stretches to 2/3 width and looks like a card).
- **credit-notes-detail**: not viewed separately (same RecordPage).
- **parts-shop-order**: smallest detail page: Products + Order card + Activity. Primary actions `Ship` (primary) and `Cancel order` (outline) replace Edit/Delete: correct, but the pair sits in the same slot where other pages have Edit/Delete, so "Cancel order" (destructive-ish) is a neutral outline here and "Delete" is red elsewhere. Order card: "Placed by: —", "VAT €0.00" noise.
- **checklists-settings-detail**: template detail with two cards on the side, where the first (Norm / Inspection valid for) has no title and no card header (a header-less card among titled ones, and it repeats the subtitle "NEN-EN 12604/12453"). No Edit/Delete in the header at all (editing is elsewhere), unlike all other RecordPage pages. Breadcrumb has 3 levels ("Work orders > Checklist templates > Sectionaaldeur").
- **checklists-work-order**: NOT a RecordPage: a printed "document" page. Hand-rolled h1 ("Work order ORD-…" text-2xl) inside a centred max-w-3xl card, back link as arrow + number (no breadcrumbs), a single Print button top right, outside the card. Uses small uppercase eyebrow labels (CUSTOMER, SITE, ENGINEER) that exist nowhere else in the office app (the labels elsewhere are muted sentence case). Fine as a print sheet, but it sits in the app shell with the full sidebar.
- **orders-new / customers-new / equipment-new / contracts-new / materials-new / invoices-new**: same skeleton: breadcrumbs "X > New", h1, one-line description, full-width section cards (teal bar + title + subtitle, divider, 2-col field grid), Cancel (ghost) + primary button right-aligned below the last card. Good and consistent. Differences: see 4 to 9.
- **orders-edit, customers-edit, ...** not looked at separately; same RecordForm.
- **credit-notes-new**: card WITHOUT a section header, max-w-2xl, so it's the odd one out: narrower than its siblings, header-less card, Cancel/Create right-aligned at 2xl width (buttons at x=950 rather than at the page's right edge x=1415).
- **invoices-credit**: a full-height dashed empty box (about 700 px) with centred "Nothing to credit". The empty state is a page-sized card with a dashed border; no other page does that. (Here it is the state of a draft invoice; the page should really not be reachable or should redirect.)
- **invoices-new**: "What to bill" card is an empty body (a header with a divider and nothing below, 40 px blank) until a customer is chosen. Customer select is `max-w` 450px inside a full-width card (half width), whereas the equivalent select in orders-new is half-width of a 2-col grid, and in equipment-new the Customer select is full width: three widths for the same field.

## 2. Numbered inconsistencies

1. **Delete / destructive action looks different per page (high).** RecordPage `remove` gives a red-tinted button (orders, customers, engineers, equipment, contracts). Location detail: neutral outline "Delete" (equipment-location). Shop order: "Cancel order" neutral outline. Invoice: "Void" red-tinted. Majority = red tint; location and shop order should use the same destructive style, and "Cancel/Void/Delete" should be one style (record-page.svelte, form/delete-dialog.svelte).
2. **Summary tiles differ per page (high).** customers: 4 tiles with icon, label left, accent value right (row layout, 3-line subtitles); equipment: 4 stacked tiles with text-3xl figure; engineers/materials: 3 tiles stacked, text-2xl; orders, contracts, invoices, location, shop order: none. Same `StatTile layout="card"`, different density and figure sizes. Decide: either all detail pages with KPI have the equipment style, or none (the customer's row layout is the odd one and has the worst alignment). Also the accent colours (teal/amber on customers) are used for numbers only there.
3. **Detail pages with unbounded tables (high).** materials-detail (about 140 + about 60 rows, 9,649 px), engineers-detail Open orders (32 rows), contracts Covered equipment (19), customers Equipment (10) and Recent activity (10, with "View all 42 orders") are all different policies: cap + "View all" link (customers: Recent activity, equipment: Activity) vs everything (materials, engineers, contracts). Pick one cap (10) + link to the filtered list.
4. **Header actions: count, order and style (med).** Orders/customers/engineers/contracts: [Edit outline][Delete red]. Equipment location: [Print labels][Edit][Delete] all outline. Invoice: [Print][Download PDF][Void red][Issue primary]. Materials: [Receive stock][Count stock][Edit][Delete]. Shop order: [Ship primary][Cancel order]. Checklist template: none. Primary action position varies (last on invoices, first on shop order). Rule: primary first/last consistently (suggest last, nearest the edge), secondary actions grouped; with more than 3, collapse into a "..." menu.
5. **Two "Activity" cards on the customer page and a card per feed everywhere (med).** Every side column ends with an Activity card (a 150 px empty state "No activity ... yet" in most seeded records) which pushes the page down. Customers has a second "Activity log". Consider collapsing the empty state to a one-line card (or hiding below the fold), and naming the customer's second feed "Equipment activity".
6. **Layout: columns vs masonry vs document (med).** `columns` (orders, customers, engineers, contracts, materials, invoices, shop order) vs `masonry` (equipment only) vs a hand-built document (work order). Masonry gives ragged blocks and reads in a different order. Majority = columns (2/3 + 1/3). Equipment could use columns with the timeline in main and facts in side, or tabs-free grouping ("Maintenance" group).
7. **Key/value grids wrap badly (med).** Customer, Engineer, Owner cards (orders, equipment, contracts) use a 2-column dl in a 1/3 column: long e-mail addresses break mid-word ("…exampl/e"). Use 1 column for email, or `break-all`->`truncate` + mailto link. Same value renders as a link in some cards (location, order list) and plain text in others (customer name and codes in the order's Customer card are not links!). Majority: plain text. Better: link the record names.
8. **Section card titles inconsistent in structure (med).** Header-less card (checklist template "Norm"), card with title only and no subtitle (order's Schedule/Equipment, Parts "Order"), title+subtitle (most), title+subtitle+button (Customer card's `Add site`, Condition, Components). Lagoon renders the teal bar on all, but the header height changes (title only 49 px vs 70 px) so adjacent cards in a row/masonry have unequal headers. Always supply a one-line description or use the compact style for all.
9. **Form widths (med).** All RecordForm pages are full width (about 1140 px at 1440: label and field at either end of a screen, 2-col grid with 560 px fields, fine on 1440 but stretched on 1920+). Credit-notes/new is `max-w-2xl` (credit-notes/new/+page.svelte:41) and header-less. Invoice new select is half width. Pick a max-w (e.g. `max-w-4xl`) in RecordForm for all or none.
10. **Form section grids leave holes (low-med).** orders-new Work card: Customer + Type, then Priority alone at left with empty right cell, then full-width Description (holes at 50% right). customers-new: Country alone left; equipment-new: serial hint under the left column pushes the 2nd row down. Hints below one field make rows of unequal height and the fields in the second column misalign (contracts-new: Starts-on has no hint, Ends-on has one, so the next row starts at the hint's height; materials-new: "Opening stock" under Unit leaves right cell empty). Fix: put hints inside the grid cell with reserved height or group fields into logical rows (3-col grids).
11. **Field heights/fills.** Plain inputs have a grey fill (lagoon "muted input fills") while Select triggers have a white fill with a border (compare "Name" vs "Segment" in customers-new). Date inputs grey, native date format "dd/mm/yyyy" regardless of Dutch/English. Reads as two kinds of control side by side in every form. Majority = grey fill for inputs; Select should match (lagoon.ts input/select slots).
12. **Sticky footer vs trailing buttons (low-med).** orders-new is 1,440 px tall: Cancel / Create sit at the very bottom (also on mobile after 4 cards) and are not sticky; nothing visible when you are in the first card. A sticky bar for forms taller than a viewport (orders, equipment) would help; keep static for short forms.
13. **Inline forms in a detail page (invoices-detail) (med).** Add a line, Terms, Add to invoice: three card-forms in the side column, each with its own outline button: no relation to RecordForm, and the "Description" label is bold 16px with taller labels than every other form (label font size differs: invoice side cards use `font-medium text-base`-looking labels vs sm in RecordForm). Needs one inline-form card style.
14. **Mobile detail layout (med).** Everything stacks in source order: main (big tables) first, facts last (Schedule on an order at y=870, Customer at 1000). Tables are clipped without scroll cue (service history). Suggest: put the facts card (Schedule/Terms/Profile) directly after the header on mobile (`order-first` for the first side card), and wrap tables in `overflow-x-auto` with a fade.
15. **Header title area (low).** Order detail: status + priority badge + type as bare icon-text on the title row; equipment: status + warranty badges; contract: status + level; customer: segment badge. Types are text on orders but badges elsewhere (type = icon + plain text next to badges looks like a third element kind). Badge count 1-3 and the subtitle conventions differ (order: description; customer: "CODE · City"; equipment: model · serial · category; engineers: region). Fine, but document it.
16. **Empty states inside cards (low-med).** orders: table with "No materials on this order." row; contracts: "No orders / The contract hasn't started yet." (centred, title + body, 100 px); invoices: Activity "No activity" centred; orders Invoice "It can be invoiced once it's completed." left-aligned text; equipment components: left text; credit page: full-page dashed Empty. At least four empty-state styles inside cards on detail pages (centred titled, left plain text, table row, dashed page). Majority = centred "title + sentence" from `Empty`.
17. **Document pages: invoice and work order (low-med).** Invoice detail is a card on 2/3 width; work order is a centred 3xl card with a back arrow and Print outside. Neither resembles the other; both are "documents" that print. A shared `DocumentSheet` wrapper (max width, header with company name, meta block right-aligned as in both) would make them the same: both already have the same header idea (SHLTR, meta block top right with Issued/Due | Type/Status/Date/Engineer): that part is a good pattern.
18. **Breadcrumb "New" and Edit titles (low).** New pages use breadcrumb "Entity > New", edit pages title "Edit {record}" with number or serial ("Edit A28XK6BQSK" for a unit named Airco AC-01 whereas the detail title is the name and the materials edit title is "Edit Bodemafdichting 3 m · SD-004"). Unit edit should say the name, not the serial; the three tab titles follow three patterns (see slugs equipment-edit, materials-edit, customers-edit).
19. **Role of the teal accent (low).** Only 3 solid teal elements on detail pages (Issue, Ship, Print on work order) vs always on forms (Create button). Visual weight fine, but on RecordForm pages the teal primary button is the ONLY teal element besides tiny bars, so the form looks grey/white-heavy; the bars before card titles are 2 px high and almost invisible at 100%.

## 3. Worth keeping

- `RecordPage` as the one way: breadcrumbs, badges, subtitle, 2/3+1/3 columns, activity last. It is the best structural idea in the app.
- Section cards with the teal bar, subtitle and divider (lagoon) read well and group form fields; keep.
- The order detail's status/priority badges and the compact "label above value" key/value cards (Schedule card with 3 columns is the best example).
- Equipment's service-history timeline (icon rail, dates, status badges) is the nicest component in this scope; the Condition card's progress bar + history is also good. Reuse the timeline for order/customer activity.
- The invoice sheet's header with meta block, and the work order's signature boxes.
- StatTile "stacked, big number" (equipment) is clearer than the row layout (customers).
- Form footers: ghost Cancel + primary right-aligned.

## 4. Recommendations

1. One destructive-action rule in RecordPage: Delete/Cancel/Void use the same red tint; move 3+ actions into a "..." menu; primary action always last.
2. Cap every list card on detail pages at 10 rows with "View all" (materials usage and stock history, engineers' open orders, contract equipment), so no detail page exceeds about 2 viewports.
3. Make one summary-tile style (stacked, text-2xl) and use it on all detail pages that have KPIs; remove the row layout from customers.
4. Give RecordForm a shared max width (or none) and fix grids: logical rows, hint height reserved, equal fill for select and input; add a sticky action bar for tall forms. Bring credit-notes/new and invoices-new in line (section header, full width).
5. Mobile: facts card first, tables scroll horizontally with a cue; shorten empty Activity cards; add a `DocumentSheet` for invoice and work-order pages.
